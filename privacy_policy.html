Privacy Policy for Save the Eye

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Save the Eye ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles user information and data. The Game is an indie physics puzzle game developed using the Godot Engine.

Key Summary: We respect your privacy. Save the Eye does not collect, store, or share any personal data on developer servers. All gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The Game is designed to be fully playable without requiring personal identification:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in with any email, social media, or username.
  • No Personal Details: We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, address, contacts, or photos.
  • No Proprietary Servers: We do not operate external database servers that log or track your gameplay sessions.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores data strictly in local storage on your device (user:// storage partition):

  • Gameplay Progress: Completed level numbers, stars/candies collected, and unlocked episodes.
  • Audio & Display Preferences: Music volume, sound effects volume, and mute settings.
  • Custom Levels: Levels created by you in the in-game editor (planks, pins, geometry).

This data never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time by clearing the application cache/data in Android system settings or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support the development and maintenance of the Game, we integrate trusted third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC):
    The Game uses Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads (for example, allowing the player to voluntarily choose to watch an ad to receive a gameplay hint). Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information such as:
    • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID)
    • IP address (for coarse geolocation and fraud prevention)
    • App performance metrics and crash diagnostics
    This data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more by visiting: Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you choose to make in-app purchases (such as unlocking additional level packs or episodes):

  • All transactions are processed securely directly through Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC).
  • We do not receive, store, or process your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses.

5. Device Permissions

The Game requests only the minimal necessary Android permissions required for advertising network connectivity:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required by Google AdMob to fetch and display ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to check whether an active internet connection is available.

The Game does not access your camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or external storage files.

6. Children's Privacy

Save the Eye is a general audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us so we can investigate and address it.

7. Data Security

We care about protecting your local gameplay data. Local save files are stored securely within your device's isolated application sandbox and can be encrypted to prevent unauthorized modification or tampering.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
